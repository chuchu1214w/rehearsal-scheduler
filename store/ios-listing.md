## 描述(App Store)
Season 是舞团内部使用的排练排程工具:

- 成员在手机上涂格子填写空闲时间,两分钟填完一周
- 管理员一键排程:自动满足"同一曲目的人必须同时在场"等硬性规则,并尽量减少来回奔波
- 排练表按课程表式周日历展示,点日期看当天日程,可切换"我的"和"全体"
- 排练表发布、排练地点更新、明天的排练,都会推送到手机
- 一键订阅到系统日历,变动自动更新

本 App 仅供受邀的舞团成员使用:账号由管理员开通,没有自助注册。

- 备注(英文,直接粘贴):

  Season is an internal scheduling app for a single ~14-member dance club. Accounts are created by the club administrator on the web console; there is no self sign-up, no payments and no public content. Members use the app to submit their weekly availability, view the rehearsal timetable, and receive native push notifications (APNs) when the schedule or rehearsal venue changes. We intend to distribute this app as an Unlisted app (link-only) after approval. Demo account credentials are provided above.
